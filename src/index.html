The honest fine print

  • We do not promise that anyone will raise money. Nobody honest can. We pay back founders who do the work and get the result.
  • You set your three targets in Steps 1 and 2. After Step 2 they lock and can only go up.
  • Money counts only when the funder confirms it and it is in your bank. Money from friends and family counts, but at most half of the total. Your own money does not count.
  • Sales must come from real customers (at least 70% strangers), checked by an accountant. Profit is stated by an accountant after every cost.
  • A person audits every claim before any refund is paid. A false claim ends the refund. The refund goes back to the payment method you used.
  • Payment-gateway fees on the original payment are not refunded by the gateway.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need an MVP, an app or a team first?

No. To raise funds you do not need an MVP, a working product, a PPT, an Excel sheet, a website, an app, a team or even money. You need to be clear on the 4 questions.

How is payment made?

By UPI, card or net banking, through Razorpay. The payment is made to IT Services by Rajat Agarwal. No GST is charged. You get a confirmation e-mail with your private access link straight away, and Razorpay also sends its own receipt.

What if money was taken but I got no confirmation?

Write to rajatsiroffice@gmail.com with your payment ID, or call {{PHONE_DISPLAY}}. A wrong or double charge is always put right.

How many startups can join?

Only 10 seats are available each month, so batches stay small. You can book up to 5 seats for your startups in one payment.

What does "win" mean, exactly?

Before you start, you set three targets: how much outside money you will raise, your monthly sales, and your profit margin. Raise a quarter of your funding target by Step 9 and half your payment comes back. Reach half of each of the three by Step 12 and all of it does. Other people prove every number: funders, an accountant and your customers.

Can you guarantee I will raise funds?

No, and nobody honest can. The money is a funder's decision. What we promise is simple: if you do the work and hit the targets, you get your payment back. If you do not, you keep what you learned.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything you enter is locked on your own device with a key made from your passphrase. We store only the locked copy and cannot read it. See the privacy notice.

Where does the work happen?

On your own phone or laptop. Our servers only store the locked copy and pass messages, so the product costs you nothing extra and keeps working at your pace.
